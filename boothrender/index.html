Info reel: “Find your people.”

What blueLink is, in 38 seconds, for people who have never heard of it. Deep Field look: one camera flies through a night sky where every star is an Atenean, and the app screens hang on the stars. The music and sound effects were made for this reel, so there is nothing to license.

  1. 0:00The hook: “This is you.” The camera pulls back and the sky fills with Ateneans, most of whom you haven’t met yet. Lines draw between the stars while it says “blueLink helps you meet them.”
  2. 0:08The two links lock together into the logo, set big like a poster, then “Welcome to Atenean Networking.”
  3. 0:12Three questions, cut on the beat: “Want connections?”, “Open to work?”, and “Good at something?”

  6. 0:29₱99 a year or ₱250 for life, and a full refund within 7 days.
  7. 0:33The planet’s edge rises: “Find your people.”, the link in bio, and the line that it isn’t an official Ateneo service.

Caption

Every star in this video is an Atenean, and you probably haven’t met most of them yet. blueLink is an app for Ateneo students, alumni, and faculty to find each other. Post that you’re looking for work, ask an upperclassman for advice, or let people know what you’re good at. It’s ₱99 a year or ₱250 for life. If you change your mind within 7 days, we’ll give you a full refund. The link’s in our bio. Made by Ateneans. Not an official Ateneo de Manila University service.

How a sale works

Online payment is not on yet. At the booth they pay ₱99 (1 year) or ₱250 (lifetime) in cash or GCash, and get a voucher card with a code. They type the code under “Have a code?” on the join screen.

      What we know

      • Price: ₱99 a year or ₱250 for lifetime. Same app on both, no premium tier. App, shared/membership.js
      • Who can join: Ateneo emails only (student.ateneo.edu, obf.ateneo.edu, ateneo.edu). App, shared/school.js
      • Codes: look like ACD-FGH. Each works once. A lifetime code gives lifetime access; a 1-year code gives 365 days on top of any time left. Admins make them in the app: Admin → Voucher cards → pick Lifetime or 1 year → Make codes → Save codes (a .txt). Then npm run cards -- codes.txt prints the cards. App, Admin and Join
      • Headline: “Welcome to Atenean Networking.” Sign-off: “Find your people.” BRAND.md
      • On every print: “Made by Ateneans. Not an official Ateneo de Manila University service.” No Ateneo logo, seal, or Blue Eagle. No “18+” on visitor pieces. Legal check
      • Never on a print: promises of replies or jobs, member counts, testimonials, “free”, raffles, or prizes. Legal check
      • Look: Deep Field. Night sky, sharp corners, one blue word. BRAND.md

      Still to check

      • Domain: every print and QR goes to bluelinkph.com. Make sure the site is live before printing, then test the printed QR on 2 phones.
      • Receipts and permission: ask the entrep coordinator if the sales need official receipts (BIR), and if the school allows selling memberships at the bazaar.
      • Privacy notice: it must be live at bluelinkph.com/#/privacy before printing. Ask Ateneo’s Data Protection Office to look at it.
      • Voucher terms: the refund contact and the valid-until date print as “[… — to confirm]”. Fill in F.refundContact and F.validUntil in scripts/print/kit.mjs.
      • Founding member cards: the price paid is a blank to write in. Decide the price, or if these are given away (then no “price paid” line, and no “free” wording).
      • Event: the venue, the dates, the stall, the fee, and the tent size.
      • Team: who staffs the booth and the shifts. Not on this page yet.
      • Standee and header: the standee is 2 × 5 ft on an X-stand. The header (optional) is 3.5 × 1.25 ft: measure the eave first. With a 2.0 m eave its bottom edge is at 1.62 m. Get 2 quotes.
      • Table: who brings it, and its size.
      • GCash: the GCash name and number in the app’s Admin settings, and a printed GCash QR.
      • Testing: sign in, pay, and redeem one code before the first day. Test the printed QR on 2 phones.
