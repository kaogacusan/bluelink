06 · Rules

Words and rules

Plain, short, warm. Talk like a helpful upperclassman. Say only what is true and what the app really does.

The words

  • The private network where asking is normal.
  • Find your people.
  • ₱99 for a year, or ₱250 for lifetime. Both plans get the same app. No premium tier.

Legal lines, on every public piece

  • Not official: “Made by Ateneans. Not an official Ateneo de Manila University service.”
  • Age: BlueLink is 18+ (it is in the app’s terms, and staff check it at the table), but “18+” never goes on a visitor piece: it reads as NSFW. Say “For college students, alumni, and faculty.”
  • Privacy: “We never sell your data.” and the privacy notice link.
  • Money: the price, what it gives, the 7-day refund. A voucher also says “not an official receipt”.
  • At least 8 pt on print.

Do

  • Keep dark pieces really dark. The sky is near black, not bright blue.
  • One blue word in a headline.
  • Hairlines, not shadows. Corners 0–4 px.
  • Plate marks on frames. Mono labels for small facts.

Don’t

  • Promise replies, jobs, internships, or results.
  • Show member counts, made-up members, or testimonials.
  • Use the Ateneo seal, logo, Blue Eagle, or official fonts.
  • Say “free”, or run a raffle or a prize.
  • Round corners past 4 px, glassy blobs, neon, or purple-pink gradients.
